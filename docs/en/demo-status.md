# Sepolia and Base Sepolia demo data

This page presents Ethereum Sepolia and Base Sepolia totals as two rows of one table and combines Tamagaki SBTs and contribution history from both chains into shared views. The Tamagaki view moves from a castle-wide overview to 100-token districts and individual artwork. Supporters can find their Tamagaki by connecting a wallet or searching, then create a permanent share link. Chain identity remains attached to every item.
